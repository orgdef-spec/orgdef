orgdef.org

The open standard for organization-chart definitions.

orgdef is a portable, open format for describing organizations: the positions in an org chart, the relationships between them (reports-to, peer-of, derives-from, validates-for, implements-for, coordinates-with), the incumbents currently occupying each position (human, AI session, vacant), and the authority boundaries that govern the org. One org chart per artifact; positions reference roledef entries by id+url for the roles they implement.

Authority, not tasks

orgdef captures who has what authority and how positions relate — not who is doing what task. Task tracking is out of scope; structure is the whole job.

AI-native incumbents

A position's incumbent can be a human, an AI session arc, or vacant — first-class. Roles are role-portable (per the linked roledef); seats can change occupants without rewriting the org.

Composable with roledef

Each position carries an optional role_definition reference (id + version + url) pointing at a roledef. The org chart says where the role lives; the roledef says what it is.

Renders for free

orgdef artifacts are catdef .openthing files. The reference renderer at render.catdef.org auto-detects namespaced types and renders orgdef artifacts as detail views today; graph layout for charts is on the v0.2 roadmap.

File Format

orgdef artifacts use the catdef substrate's universal formats:

.openthing — single org definition (type: "orgdef:Organization")
.opencatalog — a catalog of orgs (type: "orgdef:Library")

OAGP Family

orgdef is part of the Open Agentic Governance Pattern family of open standards:

catdef roledef orgdef memodef
